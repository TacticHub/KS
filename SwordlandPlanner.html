📖 Planner's Guide - Step by Step
How to set up a legion, build the roster, and share it in game and on Discord ▼
Set up
1
Choose a legion and time
Open the Legion 1 or Legion 2 tab. Set the start time in UTC (your local time shows beside it) and add any rally notes.
2
Choose how to assign
In Assign By, pick:
  • Groups - players go to Group 1, 2, or 3
  • Buildings - each player also gets a specific building
  • Both - buildings, posted under each group
3
Inside / Outside Optional
Turn on Inside / Outside Strategy if you're using it. Players get tagged I (stay in the safe zone, garrison everything) or O (roam out to capture, back before 10:00).
Build the roster
4
Add players
Tap ＋ Add Player on a group card, Upload Excel / CSV to fill both legions at once, or paste names and power into Auto Assign to have groups, roles, and buildings picked for you. Imported DPS and Farm/Support players are sorted automatically; imported tanks and healers all land in Group 1, so check that card after importing. Players waiting for an opening go on the Substitutes card (add a Subs row in your sheet to import them there).
5
Assign roles
For each player set the Role: Lead (or Tank 1-3), Healer, DPS, or Farm/Support. Then set Building and I / O if those are turned on. The map sidebar lists each building's points and perks. On the Substitutes card, pick the group each sub backs up.
Share
6
In-game chat
Tap 💬 Copy on a group card to copy that group, or 💬 Copy In-Game Chat for the whole legion. Paste each block as its own message in your legion chat. Leads go on the Lead: line; everyone else is listed with (I) or (O).
7
Discord
Tap 📋 Generate Discord Output, then Copy to Clipboard and paste into your Discord channel. For a picture, use 🖼 Visual Export and screenshot it or print to PDF.
8
Save the plan
Your legions auto-save to the shared live plan. Tap 💾 Save Plan to also keep a copy in the cloud archive. Open 📚 Archive to load, export, or delete past plans. Saved plans are shared with everyone who uses this planner.
✅ Your legions save automatically. Each legion is one shared live plan: every change saves within a couple of seconds, and every planner on any device sees the same plan. It stays until someone taps Clear Legion. Use 💾 Save Plan to keep a copy in the archive.

The strongest players (about 1 in 6) become the DPS strike team: they capture buildings, hand them to the lead, and move on. The strongest of them takes Swordshrine at 15:00. Every building gets a lead. Extra players go to Sanctum and the Abbeys first (up to lead + 4), while the perk buildings get lead + 1 until those are full. The next strongest lead each building and everyone else supports; Group 2's two lowest-power supporters become the Loot crew. Only 30 players can join; anyone past 30 goes to Substitutes.

📝 Notes:
🗺 Map Overview
Group 1 - Bell Tower, Top Abbey, Mercenary
Group 2 - Sanctum, Mid Abbey
Group 3 - Low Abbey, Reformation, Stables
Swordshrine - Contested Center
⚔ TANKS / LEAD
  • Teleport to the assigned building(s)
  • Get it first (go solo if needed)
  • Attack enemies that show up (solo or rally with your healers)
  • Main march on your building; send secondary marches to your group's other buildings
✚ HEALERS
  • Teleport to the assigned building(s)
  • Garrison in both assigned buildings if possible
  • Reinforce your tank / reinforce each other
  • Use the "Share Coordinates" option in chat so DPS can quickly get to you
  • Send secondary marches to your group's thinnest buildings first
  • Loot crew (Group 2): collect Baggage Trains when we flip an enemy building; gather at the Undercellars in the 15:00 and 20:00 waves
  • Phase 2: Group 1 holds the enemy Abbey and Group 3 holds the enemy Sanctum once the strike team takes them
🎯 DPS
  • Strike team: rally and capture buildings, then hand them off to the building lead
  • Roam free
  • Do not garrison (you will move a lot) - teleporting pulls your marches out
  • After a capture, stay until the lead's marches arrive, then move on
  • Phase 2: once our buildings hold, rally the enemy Sanctum, then the enemy Abbey
  • Phase 3: hit the enemy building with the most banked points so the loot crew can collect
  • Teleport to any position your help is needed
  • Attack every player lower than you
  • Be a pain in their ass
  • Demotivate their team
  • Burn players that come on our side of the map
  • Steal first capture points from the enemy
